General Information

The EnterpriseSG Open Innovation Challenge connects Singapore-based solution providers with EnterpriseSG's demand-driver partners to solve real, validated operational problems. This edition is run with Meridian Logistics Pte Ltd on the theme of Smart Logistics & Supply Chain Resilience.

Open to Singapore-registered companies and individuals forming a team. No fee to participate. Shortlisted teams pilot their solution with Meridian's operations team; EnterpriseSG co-funds the pilot for winning teams.

Challenge Statements

Forecast yard congestion 4-12 hours ahead using gate-in/out and berth schedule data, so yard planners can re-sequence moves before a bottleneck forms.

Data provided: 12 months of anonymized gate and berth schedule logs.

Pilot scope: Meridian's Pasir Panjang yard, 8-week trial with the winning team.

Re-plan last-mile delivery routes in real time against live traffic and delivery-window changes for a fleet of ~120 vehicles.

Data provided: Historical route and delivery-window data for 3 distribution hubs.

Pilot scope: Meridian's East Coast distribution hub, 8-week trial with the winning team.

Apply for CS2

Surface upstream supplier and shipping-lane risk (weather, port congestion, customs delays) as an early-warning feed for ops planners.

Data provided: Supplier and shipping-lane reference data; public weather and port-status feeds.

Pilot scope: Meridian's ops planning team, 8-week trial with the winning team.

Rules & Regulations

01

Eligibility

Open to Singapore-registered entities and individuals; teams of up to 5.

02

Submission Requirements

A working demo, a short solution write-up, and a 3-minute video walkthrough - one submission per team per challenge statement.

03

Deadline Policy

No submissions accepted after the deadline. EnterpriseSG may extend it at its discretion, posted here and emailed to applicants.

04

Data & Privacy

Proposals and any personal data collected for the challenge are deleted from the platform 1 year after the submission deadline.

05

IP & Licensing

IP in each submission remains with the submitting team; Meridian Logistics receives a license to pilot shortlisted solutions.

06

Full Terms & Conditions
