Rules & Regulations

What's Required

  • Eligibility: Open to Singapore-registered entities and individuals; teams of up to 5.
  • Submission: A working demo, a short solution write-up, and a 3-minute video walkthrough - one submission per team per challenge statement.
  • Deadline: No submissions accepted after the deadline. EnterpriseSG may extend it at its discretion, posted here and emailed to applicants.

Good to Know

  • Data & Privacy: Proposals and any personal data collected for the challenge are deleted from the platform 1 year after the submission deadline.
  • IP & Licensing: IP in each submission remains with the submitting team; Meridian Logistics receives a license to pilot shortlisted solutions.
