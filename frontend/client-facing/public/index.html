About this challenge

Meridian Logistics operates container yards and last-mile distribution hubs across Singapore. Congestion, delivery delays, and limited real-time visibility into upstream risk are costing the business time and money - and slowing its customers down. EnterpriseSG is running this Open Innovation Challenge to connect Meridian with solution providers who can prototype a working fix against one of three problem statements below, with the strongest submissions piloted directly in Meridian's operations.
