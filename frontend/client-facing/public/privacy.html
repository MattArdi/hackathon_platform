Privacy & Data Retention Notice

This notice covers information collected through the EnterpriseSG Open Innovation Challenge platform for the Smart Logistics & Supply Chain Resilience challenge.

What we collect

Registration details, team and proposal content, uploaded documents, and any messages sent through the platform's chat feature.

Who can see it

Your proposal is visible only to Meridian Logistics (for the challenge statement you applied to) and EnterpriseSG. It is never made public.

Retention

Proposals and any personal data collected to facilitate your submission are deleted from the Contractor's database 1 year after the submission deadline (6 Mar 2027).

Questions

Contact oichallenge@enterprisesg.gov.sg or use the chat feature from My Dashboard.
